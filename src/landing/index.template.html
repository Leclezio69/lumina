FOR THE GROWN-UPS

Less guessing.
More understanding.

Created with children with ADHD and autistic children in mind. Open to every explorer, with choices for different interests and sensory preferences.

LUMINA is a play experience, not a treatment or assessment. It has not been clinically evaluated or playtested with children.

What does my child actually do?＋

Start by planting, watering, and growing one flower. Then explore flower patterns, connected water tiles, lantern sequences, and a garden that changes through time. Every adventure has visible instructions and free help.

Who is it for?＋

The current activities are aimed roughly at ages 6–10, with adult help where useful. That is a design starting point, not an assessment of any child’s ability. Let their interests, understanding, and comfort guide the session.

What happens if an answer is different?＋

There are no lost lives, countdowns, or penalties. Correct steps stay completed. A hint can show the next shape or help turn a water tile.

Does it collect my child’s information?＋

The game has no account, advertising, purchases, chat, or analytics. It asks for no child’s name or diagnosis. Progress stays in this browser when storage is available. Optional read-aloud uses the browser’s speech service, which may have its own processing.

How do I open and play it?＋

Download this HTML file and open it in Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox on a computer. Select “Play now” to enter the included game. Some file-preview apps do not run games. The detailed scene artwork works without 3D; the optional 3D view requires browser WebGL support. Device and browser performance have not yet been verified.

Can we stop halfway through?＋

Yes. Pause whenever you like. The game saves discoveries and your place when browser storage is available. Private browsing, browser changes, or moving between local files may affect saved progress.
